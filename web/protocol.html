Payment and payout

A payment includes a shielded merchant output and a service fee. The merchant checks its output; the service validates the transaction and checks its fee.

Merchant requests use JSON-RPC. Miners fetch the pending set over Zakura v2 QUIC every 500 ms. Acceptance is signed before inclusion; payout waits for the inclusion block and one successor.

Conflicting payments

A and B spend the same shielded note. A higher network fee or earlier mempool arrival does not override the service’s acceptance.

This priority applies to participating miners. An outsider can mine B instead; A then fails and earns no payout. The receipt is not a consensus guarantee.

Accounting and recovery

Each included transaction earns its miner 98% of its service fee. Fees are summed per block, rounded down to whole zatoshis, and paid in one transaction. For example: 30 payments × 10,000 zat = 300,000 zat collected; 294,000 zat goes to the miner. The service funds the payout’s network fee separately.

Blocks are attributed to configured Ironwood coinbase receivers using the public zero outgoing viewing key. Unknown or ambiguous recipients receive no automatic payout.

Duplicate submissions return the original receipt. Payout assignments and signed transaction bytes survive restarts. If inclusion is rolled back before signing, its payout is canceled and still-valid payments return to pending. Reorgs affecting a signed payout are held for inspection rather than paid again.
